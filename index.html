What TWH provides

    What the process is designed to support

      The process supports board deliberation and progress towards an agreed strategy. Final decisions, further refinements and formal approval remain the responsibility of the client board. Timeframes depend on timely client participation.

      TĀ MĀTOU AROMATAWAI | How we assess strategy

      We look at more than the words on the page.

      Our strategy reviews consider readiness, completeness, coherence, measures and implementation. The example below illustrates the types of questions we explore — not a finding about any particular organisation.

      A STRATEGY QUALITY LENS
      ReadinessStrong foundation
      CompletenessSome gaps
      CoherenceGenerally coherent
      Success measuresNeeds development
      ImplementationFurther work needed
      Illustrative assessment only. These are not client findings, measured scores or actual results.

      Questions that lead to better decisions

      Is the strategic direction clear? Do the priorities reflect the people you serve? Are goals realistic, measurable and owned? How will the board know whether the strategy is working?

      We use a structured review to identify strengths, gaps and useful areas for board deliberation.

      Thoughtful strategy is shaped through people, dialogue and purposeful decisions.
